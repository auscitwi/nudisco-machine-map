What is NUDISCO?

NUDISCO is a public site for personal writing and unusual web experiences. The normal pages favor a restrained, Linux-terminal-like design: monospace text, blue structural labels, quiet green telemetry, and very little interface chrome.

Three public experiences

  • The signal: a public feed of writing, with literal search, filters, and a LOAD MORE control.
  • Astra: an intentionally anomalous magenta signal inside an otherwise calm interface.
  • The PUBLIC VOID: a separate interactive experience with moving text. Some words link back to the site's search.
